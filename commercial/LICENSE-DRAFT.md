# Commercial Kit license — DRAFT

> Draft for owner and legal review. Not in force. Until it is approved, the
> repository-level MIT license is the only license that applies to this folder.

Proposed terms for a buyer of the Creator Launch OS Commercial Kit:

1. **Use.** The buyer may use and modify the kit's playbooks, templates and schemas for their own products and for client work they deliver.
2. **No resale.** The buyer may not resell, sublicense or publicly redistribute the kit, alone or as the main value of another product.
3. **Updates.** The buyer receives updates to the kit's major version they bought.
4. **Refund ends the license.** A refunded purchase ends the right to future updates and further use of unmodified kit files.
5. **Free core unaffected.** The Creator Launch OS source code outside `commercial/` stays under MIT, whether or not someone buys the kit.
6. **No warranty.** The kit is provided as-is. It is operating guidance, not legal, tax or financial advice.

Open questions for the owner: the legal entity named as licensor, governing law, and whether option 1 or 2 in `commercial/README.md` applies.
