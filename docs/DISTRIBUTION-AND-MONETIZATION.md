# Distribution and Monetization

Creator Launch OS is a multi-product storefront template, not a single-product business. The free repository proves the deployment and catalog experience. Commercial value should live in clearly separated, supportable extensions rather than hidden restrictions in the MIT core.

## Product ladder

1. Free core: deployable storefront, typed catalog, honest checkout handoff and sample studio.
2. Launch Kit: brand setup, product-manifest pack, marketplace listing pack, real telemetry schema and release playbook.
3. Commerce Pack: one reviewed provider implementation for orders, webhooks, entitlements, secure delivery, refunds and reconciliation.
4. Studio Updates: maintained compatibility, new channel adapters, evals and release operations.
5. Team License: shared catalogs, roles, approvals and evidence for a small product studio.

The public price remains undecided until the extension's support, license, merchant, refund and end-to-end fulfillment are ratified.

## Channel order

- GitHub and a verified Vercel deployment prove the free core.
- The owned GenCreator/FrankX offer page is the canonical commercial promise.
- Vercel Templates is a discovery target for the zero-secret starter.
- Gumroad or another approved Merchant of Record may validate the cross-format kit after fee-aware margin review.
- Framer, Webflow, Notion and Canva receive native products only; they are not destinations for an unchanged Next.js repository.

Every channel variant is generated from marketplace.listing.yaml and receives an explicit platform ID, URL, version, price state, license, submission and verification receipt.

## Release gate

Do not claim a paid kit until a buyer can understand the offer, pay through an authorized test, receive the correct entitlement and files, reach the first useful result, request support/refund, reconcile the order and export their data without founder rescue.
