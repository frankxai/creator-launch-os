# From templates to useful product families

Research date: 2026-09-14. This is a ranked research and implementation backlog, not a price announcement, marketplace submission or revenue forecast. It extends the existing product contract. Current implementation: [standalone project export](TEMPLATE-PROJECT-EXPORT.md).

## Commercial thesis

Sell saved work and dependable outcomes. A strong design earns attention; editable content, real integrations, clear licensing, reliable installation and ongoing compatibility make the purchase useful. Templates can introduce a family of buyer-owned tools without requiring another hosted platform.

The immediate reuse is the existing six-composition atelier. Independent source projects close a delivery gap. That does not establish demand for six paid products. Choose one commercial direction after buyer research and a cold activation trial.

Do not confuse platform users, installations, downloads or gross merchandise volume with our obtainable paying market. Evidence collected here does not establish category GMV, typical seller earnings or conversion rates. Estimate obtainable demand from measured category exposure, visits, activation, purchases and retention.

Arithmetic only: 20 sales at €99 are €1,980 gross; 100 are €9,900. Approximately 10,102 sales at that price exceed €1 million before costs. At an assumed 2% purchase rate, 20 sales need 1,000 qualified visits. These are scenarios, not observed conversion or promises of weekend income.

Contribution per sale equals realized price minus platform/payment fees, refunds, variable API/storage costs, support time and acquisition costs. BYOK reduces inference exposure but not support. Annual updates are attractive only if renewals cover maintenance. The free MIT code remains MIT even when distributed in a paid bundle.

## Research that can change the decision

For one buyer/platform, begin with ten competing products, twenty useful reviews or support threads where available, and five relevant requests. Record URL, date, category, price, complaint, workaround and whether it is actual purchase evidence. Include weaker products, positive and negative feedback, native alternatives and free substitutes. Public discussions are directional and biased.

Score repeated pain, urgency, reachable distribution, differentiation, build effort and support burden. A provisional selection threshold is five independent matching pain observations and three real buyer trials. These are experiment decisions, not statistical proof. Agent personas provide critique; they do not count as customers or willingness-to-pay evidence. Use permission-based trials and do not contact people or post without authorization.

The first trial is one complete task: export, install, enter real content, preview and finish the primary journey. Record time to first useful result, help needed, expectation mismatches, willingness to pay and reasons for declining. Narrow or stop when buyers prefer a free alternative or cannot activate from the documentation.

## Knowledge and quality architecture

Use existing product manifests, canonical listings and template-studio evidence records. Do not create another global knowledge base. The useful relations are:

`buyer job → observed pain → offer promise → capability → native format → acceptance test → evidence at revision → listing → activation → support issue → improvement`

Every factual record needs source, capture date, scope and confidence. Separate observation, hypothesis, simulated critique and completed verification. Refresh platform rules before submission. Invalidate relevant build/visual evidence when code, assets or integrations change. A checksum is provenance, not a release decision.

- Demand: relevant observed pain and real trials.
- Product: one complete journey, explicit failure/empty states and understandable setup.
- Design: a named comparator, specific art direction, real proof, quality-only refinement, actual mobile/desktop inspection, keyboard and reduced motion.
- Engineering: cold installation, complete dependencies, suitable tests, verified preview and exact source revision.
- AI: useful task, grounded answers where applicable, uncertainty behavior, injection fixtures, bounded cost and private credentials.
- Distribution: actual native format, licensed assets, accurate screenshots, coherent listing and demonstrated delivery/access/refund behavior where sold.

SEO, GEO and AEO packages should sell controllable work: crawlability, fast accessible pages, coherent entities, evidence-backed content, useful links and accurate structured data. Google's [AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) does not require special AI markup or files. Do not sell guaranteed rankings/citations or an llms.txt file as the outcome.

## Swarm and loop design

After machine admission, use three lanes plus the coordinator: a researcher for one buyer/category and disconfirming evidence; a maker with exclusive files in the chosen repo; a skeptical reviewer testing the fresh artifact from its documentation. The coordinator resolves decisions and integrates once. Use a different provider for the release critique. No duplicated writers or unbounded platform expansion.

This document creates no scheduled continuation. A later authorized loop should claim one item in the existing release system, check capacity and source revision, perform bounded work, verify, persist a receipt and exit. Cap spend and retries. Stop for stale assumptions, repeated gate failures or credential/publication needs. Notify on meaningful changes, completion, failure or required action. Never publish, change prices or contact customers from a stale PASS.
