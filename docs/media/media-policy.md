# Media Policy

Use Vercel Blob for new public, private or temporary product media and Vercel Image for responsive delivery. Do not commit large marketing masters or use the repository as an asset lake. Product content and asset references remain versioned in Git; rights and approval metadata belongs in the shared control plane; Drive may hold review packages and private source masters.

Every screenshot, demo, hero, video and downloadable asset needs a stable asset ID, version, checksum, source, rights/consent state, alt text or captions, review state and placement. Publish only approved immutable renditions. Product screenshots must show real versioned behavior and visibly label sample data.

Generated layers may not contain partner marks, factual UI, text, prices or evidence. Add exact approved elements deterministically. Motion must have a product purpose, respect reduced motion and preserve a usable static path.
